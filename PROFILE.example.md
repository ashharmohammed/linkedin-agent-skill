# My LinkedIn preferences

Public perspective: technology enthusiast observing the industry.
Audience: people curious about technology and its everyday implications.
Topics: AI products, technology adoption, consumer technology, trust/privacy, product strategy.
Avoided topics: politics; irrelevant promotion. Change these to your own preferences.
Voice: conversational plain English; one clear opinion, an accessible example, and a practical conclusion.
Personal context: include a role, employer, or firsthand experience only when I explicitly provide it for public use.
Formatting: short coherent paragraphs; no default hashtags, emoji, tagging, or promotional CTA.
Approval: show each exact version for approval before publishing. Material revisions need their own approval.

This is a starting preference template, not evidence of personal experiences or permission to publish.
